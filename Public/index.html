    Drinks

    2026 Wisconsin rule: any property rented to the public for an event now counts as a "public place" for alcohol. The venue needs either a Class B license (you buy drinks through them) or a no-sale event permit (you bring your own beer and wine, limited events per year). Ask your venue which one they have. Planning math: about 2 drinks per drinking guest in the first hour, then 1 per hour after.

    Questions to ask every venue

    • What is the price for my exact date, and is a Friday or Sunday cheaper?
    • What does the fee include: tables, chairs, linens, setup, cleanup, staff?
    • Can we bring our own caterer and our own alcohol? Is there a food or bar minimum?
    • Is the room heated and cooled? What is the rain plan for an outdoor ceremony?
    • How many guests can sit for dinner with a dance floor and DJ?
    • What time does music have to end, and when do we need to be packed out?
    • How much is the deposit, and what is the cancellation policy?
